The pair. Five sharps of quicksilver unease: a long, restless two-part prelude, then a double fugue — first subject nervous and darting; second subject a slow chromatic descent (the lament-line, F minor's old freight) — the two finally combined so the fidget and the grief are revealed as one music.

Study. Double-fugue listening in three stages: know subject A, know subject B, then attend only to their combination section — where late Bach does his deepest thinking. This one and II/14 are Book II's paired courses in multi-subject form: take both.
