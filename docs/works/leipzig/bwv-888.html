The pair. Ease itself: a pastoral prelude in gently overlapping voices and a compact three-voice fugue — ten entries, all with real answers (the same textbook transparency as its Book I sibling, a bookish rhyme across twenty years) — its dancing subject never far from the surface.

Study. Use the E-major/A-major "real answer" pairs (I/9, II/19) as your fugue-theory control group; then measure everything else's deviations against them. The books teach comparatively — Bach built the curriculum; the cards just point.
