The pair. Book II's granite: a largo prelude in dotted French gravity — trio textures over pedal points, processional weight — then a four-voice fugue on a vehement repeated-note subject: the same pitch hammered like a fist on a table before the line breaks upward, worked with unrelenting insistence to one of the book's most powerful endings.

Study. Repetition as rhetoric: a repeated-note subject risks monotony and buys inevitability — trace how the episodes vary everything around the hammering to keep it fresh. The pair's combined mass makes it Book II's answer to I/22's tragic scale.
