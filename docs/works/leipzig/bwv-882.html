The pair. The fashionable one: a dotted, French-mannered prelude, then a three-voice fugue in gavotte character that opens with a trill on the leading note — a subject beginning with an ornament, almost unprecedented in fugal writing: the galant drawing-room's most charming gesture, submitted to strict counterpoint and thriving there.

Study. With II/12's prelude, the fashion-absorption exhibit — but here the fugue itself wears the new clothes. The trill is structural (every entry announces itself by sparkle). Bach's last word to Scheibe: anything you call natural, counterpoint can carry.
