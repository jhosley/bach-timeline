Leipzig III: the summing-up

WTC II/14: Prelude and Fugue in F-sharp minor

BWV 883 · F-sharp minor ·
documented
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

The pair. Book II's inner sanctum: an arioso prelude of refined melancholy, then the book's only triple fugue — three subjects (the first austere, the second flowing, the third a chain of two-note sighs), each exposed separately, then combined in the closing pages with valedictory calm rather than triumph.

Study. The gentlest door into multi-subject architecture before the St. Anne and Art of Fugue: three ideas, one texture, no noise. Learn the three subjects by ear first; the ending then plays like three old friends agreeing at last. Many pianists' private favorite of the forty-eight.
