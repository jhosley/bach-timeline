The pair. The lean thriller of Book II: a driving prelude all sinew and momentum, then a three-voice fugue that constantly alternates duple and triple rhythms — its chromatic-tipped subject flickering between metric worlds, tension generated by time itself rather than dissonance alone.

Study. Rhythm as fugal argument: mark the two-against-three collisions and notice they intensify exactly where a Book I fugue would deploy stretto. The devices evolve; the dramaturgy is constant. D-minor storm-weather, Invention 4 to here, one lifetime.
