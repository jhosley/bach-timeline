What it is. The Morning Star: Nicolai's other great chorale (the "queen" to Wachet auf's "king") in the most radiant of the chorale-cantata fantasias — two concertante violins glittering around the hymn like the star's own scintillation, horns glowing beneath. The last new work of the great cycle's unbroken year.

Listen for. The opening's light-textures — 12/8 lilt, violins in thirds, the tune streaming in long soprano notes — and the tenor aria's dance of unforced joy.

Why it matters (Arc II edition). When the Bach-Gesellschaft began the complete edition in 1851, they opened volume one with this cantata — which is why it is BWV 1: the number is a reception-history artifact, the revival generation choosing the Morning Star as the door into the whole rediscovered continent. There are no accidents in this catalog, including its first entry.
