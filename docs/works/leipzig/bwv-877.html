The pair. Six sharps of severity: an intense two-part prelude in perpetual dialogue, then a ricercar-style four-voice fugue — the archaic, research-grade species (the word the Musical Offering resurrects) — working its subject through inversion and canon with the concentrated learning of the coming late monuments.

Study. The proto-Art-of-Fugue: hear this as a study for that project's severities, written into the "teaching" book. Compare its enharmonic sibling in Book I (eb/d#) — the same tonal place, lament then discipline.
