Leipzig III: the summing-up

WTC II/2: Prelude and Fugue in C minor

BWV 871 · C minor ·
documented
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

The pair. A flowing two-part prelude in the invention manner, then a four-voice fugue with one of the book's best structural surprises: it runs for most of its length in three voices — until the fourth voice finally enters near the end, intoning the subject in augmentation (doubled note-values), like an elder joining the conversation to have the last, slowest word.

Study. The delayed-entrance device: chart the texture's voice-count bar by bar and feel how the reserve is spent. One well-timed arrival outweighs constant fullness — Bach's economy of forces, in miniature.
