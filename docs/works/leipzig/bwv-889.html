The pair. The severe pair: a prelude that is a strict two-part invention in invertible counterpoint — its halves swapping the voices' floors wholesale (Invention 5's lesson, executed at master grade) — and a gnarled three-voice fugue whose chromatic subject and biting suspensions make it Book II's most astringent page.

Study. The invertibility audit: compare the prelude's two halves system by system — everything upstairs moves downstairs and still works. Then ask of the fugue: how much dissonance can three voices carry and remain song? Late Bach's answer is: more than you thought.
