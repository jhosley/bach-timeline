The pair. Warmth at four flats: a generous, almost orchestral prelude — dotted festivity over rolling textures — and a four-voice fugue grown (like several Book II movements) from a much earlier small fughetta, here expanded into one of the book's most spacious and singing designs: an old seed, decades later, given its full tree.

Study. The expansion habit: Book II is partly late-Bach revising young-Bach (the Great Eighteen's method inside the WTC). Where the seams should show, they don't — which is itself the lesson: his revision standard was invisibility.
