The pair. Sparkle and brevity: a moto-perpetuo prelude of violinistic figuration — one of the book's most athletic pages — balanced by a three-voice fugue so compact it is effectively a fughetta: one bright idea, stated, turned, and dismissed with perfect timing.

Study. Proportion as wit: after the prelude's extravagance, the fugue's brevity is the joke — the pair works as a unit of comic timing. A reminder that the 48 are pairs by design, not adjacency; always study both halves together.
