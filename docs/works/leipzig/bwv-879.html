Leipzig III: the summing-up

WTC II/10: Prelude and Fugue in E minor

BWV 879 · E minor ·
documented
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

The pair. A flowing two-part prelude (binary, dance-blooded), then a three-voice fugue built on the longest subject in either book — six full bars of winding, chromatic-shaded melody: less a motto than a paragraph, whose every later entry is an event simply because of its span.

Study. Subject-length as form: with a six-bar subject, episodes shrink and entries dominate — the whole fugal economy inverts. Compare the four-note I/4 subject: the two extremes of subject design, both masterpieces. (And recall I/10, E minor's other extremity — this key collects the book's outliers.)
