Core idea. Gracious B-flat amiability — the fourteenth invention's courteous conversation now seated three at the table: rounded figures, gentle imitation, the set's most companionable texture, with a soft galant sheen that points toward the style his sons would make fashionable.

Study. Voice-leading observation: follow just the middle voice through the whole piece — the least glamorous line, and the one whose good manners hold the texture together. Middle-voice awareness is the difference between playing three lines and playing three-part harmony; acquire it on this friendly ground.
