Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Sinfonia No. 10 in G major

BWV 796 · G major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. Sunlight after the f-minor abyss — the ordering is surely pedagogy of the spirit as well as the hands: buoyant G major, a nimble triadic subject, three voices at play in the set's most uncomplicated happiness.

Study. Recovery repertoire, and a balance etude: keep the dance-lightness while giving each of three voices a real presence — lightness in counterpoint is earned, never default. Compare the G-major two-part gigue: same key-character, one voice richer, how much heavier does it want to become? Resist exactly that.
