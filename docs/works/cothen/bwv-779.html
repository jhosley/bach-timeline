Core idea. The set's second-most-famous piece and its purest chase: a bugle-call arpeggio figure launches, the other hand answers one bar later, and the two pursue each other in close imitation to the finish — moto perpetuo as playground game.

Study. The gateway to hearing imitation as energy: the excitement isn't the tune, it's the pursuit — the one-bar gap that never closes. Then notice how Bach steers a chase: the sequences change direction exactly where the harmony needs to travel. Joy, fully engineered.
