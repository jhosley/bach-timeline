Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Sinfonia No. 7 in E minor

BWV 793 · E minor · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The e-minor elegy scaled up from its two-part sibling: a grieving, ornament-inflected subject moving through three voices, the texture darkening as lines cross and gather — the teaching book's first sustained tragic three-part span, preparing the greater grief two keys away.

Study. Register the difference a third voice makes to sorrow: in two parts, grief is a duet; in three it becomes a space — someone is always sustaining while others move, like held breath under speech. That sustained-voice awareness is the skill; carry it to the f-minor Sinfonia next.
