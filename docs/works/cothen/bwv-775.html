Core idea. A swirling sixteenth-note figure spun from the D-minor triad, driving and dramatic — the teaching book's first taste of the storm register (D minor is the Chaconne's and the Art of Fugue's key, and the weather starts here). The figure works as hard inverted as upright.

Study. Find where the motive flips; then notice the long dominant preparation before the final cadence — tension held deliberately. Small piece, real dramaturgy: ask where the climax is and how you know.
