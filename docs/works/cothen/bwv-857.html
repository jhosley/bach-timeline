The pair. F minor again (the Sinfonia's key of deepest grief, and the same year): a darkly weaving prelude, then a four-voice fugue on a chorale-like subject of ten slow chromatic quarter-notes spanning an octave — the lament vocabulary stated as plainly as a hymn line, then borne through four-part polyphony like a procession.

Study. Put the three 1722–23 F-minor pieces side by side — Invention, Sinfonia, this fugue: one key, three voice-counts, one grief. The best single demonstration in the catalog that for Bach a key was a place he returned to, with the same weather waiting.
