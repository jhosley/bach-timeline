The pair. Book I's first summit. An arioso prelude of veiled grief, then one of the collection's two five-voice fugues: a four-note subject whose shape traces a cross on the page (the old soggetto of sacred polyphony), joined across 115 bars by two further ideas — a flowing lament and an insistent repeated-note figure — until three subjects and five voices process together like a motet from another century.

Study. The gateway to stile antico: compare its gravity with the B minor Mass's Credo. Learn the three subjects separately, then listen for each entry — the piece rewards fifty hearings.
