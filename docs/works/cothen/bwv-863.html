The pair. Five sharps of quiet melancholy — another key made habitable by temperament and immediately furnished with feeling: an elegant duet-prelude, then an expressive four-voice fugue that (like I/13) declines learned devices; its currency is inflection — small chromatic shadings, sighing episode figures.

Study. The "device-free" fugues form their own study group (I/3, I/13, this): what remains of fugue when the toolbox stays shut? Answer: conversation and key-drama — the social essence. Understanding that makes the device-heavy fugues legible as choices, not requirements.
