Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Cello Suite No. 1 in G major

BWV 1007 · G major · 1720
disputed
Forces — Cello alone
Dating — See set card — no autograph; dating inferred.
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

What it is. The open door of the set — and, thanks to its Prelude, probably the most recognized minute of solo string music in existence. Galanterie pair: two Minuets.

Listen for. The Prelude is a single texture — rolling broken chords — with no "melody" at all, yet it feels like song. What you're hearing is pure harmony as motion: each bar one chord, spread as a wave, the top notes accidentally forming a tune. It's the friendliest possible introduction to hearing implied voices. The Sarabande shows the other side: slow, chordal, the cello as a choir of three.

Craft. Compare bar by bar with the identical-shaped preludes of WTC I (C major) — the same technique, arpeggiated harmony as complete music, in two different worlds. The pedagogy essay uses this pairing to show one idea crossing instruments.
