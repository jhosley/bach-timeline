Core idea. Taut, driving a-minor — the thirteenth invention's restless energy re-armed with a third voice: close imitation, propulsive sequences, and a sinewy insistence that made this one, like its two-part twin, a recitalist's pick.

Study. The momentum question: where does the piece breathe? Mark the few genuine cadential arrivals against the many elided ones (where the resolution instantly becomes a new departure). Elision — endings that are beginnings — is a core Bach continuity device; this piece runs on it.
