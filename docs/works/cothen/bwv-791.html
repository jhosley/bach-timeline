Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Sinfonia No. 5 in E-flat major

BWV 791 · E-flat major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The set's lyric interior: an ornamented duet for the two upper voices unfolding over a bass of quiet regularity — less an argument than an aria, E-flat warmth throughout, the most sung page in the three-part book.

Study. Treat it as vocal chamber music: name the two upper voices (soprano, mezzo?) and shape their exchanges as phrases needing breath. Bach's title page promised a cantabile style of playing — of the thirty pieces, this one is that promise's most direct test.
