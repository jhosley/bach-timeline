The pair. The terminus. A long two-voice prelude over a walking bass (Bach marks it Andante — tempo words are rare enough in the book to count as events), then the final four-voice fugue, whose crawling subject contains all twelve chromatic notes — the complete tonal universe the twenty-four keys have just mapped, folded into one grieving line, marked Largo.

Study. End-of-journey listening: the book that began in C-major daylight closes in the key of the great Mass, with total chromatic saturation. Whether that's design or destiny, it's the right final page — and the door Book II reopens twenty years later.
