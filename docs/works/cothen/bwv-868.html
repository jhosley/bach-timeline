WTC I/23: Prelude and Fugue in B major

BWV 868 · B major ·
documented
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

The pair. Five sharps of benediction before the finale's storm: a warm, compact prelude and a four-voice fugue — the only four-voice fugue of Book I cast in binary form (two halves, sectional like a dance movement): fugue and suite quietly shaking hands.

Study. Formal hybridity again, at the gentlest scale (compare the passepied-fugue and overture-fugue). And placement-listening: hear it in sequence between the massive b-flat minor and B minor pairs — the book's pacing, like a Passion's, plants calm before catastrophe.
