Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Invention No. 1 in C major

BWV 772 · C major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. A rising scale-step figure with an arpeggio turn — seven notes, and the whole piece is nothing else: traded between hands, run in sequence, and turned upside down (inversion) at the midpoint. The manifesto of the entire method: one inventio, total development.

Study. Mark every entry of the motive (there are dozens); then mark the inverted ones. When you can hear the piece as one idea's biography, you've learned what the title page meant — and you're ready for every fugue Bach wrote.
