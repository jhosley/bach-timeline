The pair. High spirits with a notational curiosity: the prelude is written under two simultaneous time signatures (the hands barred differently — 24/16 against common time), Bach notating cross-rhythm exuberance precisely. The three-voice fugue launches from a long downhill scale-run subject and never stops grinning — episodes tumbling, entries arriving like punchlines.

Study. The notation lesson: meters are descriptions of feel, and sometimes one isn't enough. Also a stamina-of-cheerfulness study for performance — comedy at length is harder than grief, in fugues as elsewhere.
