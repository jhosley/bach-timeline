Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Sinfonia No. 1 in C major

BWV 787 · C major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The third voice arrives. Same C-major world as Invention No. 1 — rising scale-figure DNA, plain daylight key — but now three lines share it, and the middle voice changes everything: no longer dialogue but society, each part taking turns as melody, companion, and bass.

Study. The Sinfonia fundamentals, learned here first: play each voice alone until it sings; then each pair; only then all three. And the harder discipline — at any moment, know which voice is the subject and which two are accompanying. That triage is three-part playing's whole secret.
