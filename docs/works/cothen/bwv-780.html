Invention No. 9 in F minor

BWV 780 · F minor · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The tragedy of the two-part set: F minor — Bach's key of deepest grief (the WTC's F-minor pair, the f-minor Sinfonia) — with chromatic descents and suspensions carrying real anguish in a children's exercise book. Two voices are enough for sorrow; that is the lesson.

Study. Trace the descending chromatic lines (the lament-bass vocabulary in miniature) and the chains of dissonance-resolution. Then the pedagogical shock: this stands three pages from the F-major chase — the affections as adjacent teachable techniques, which is the whole Baroque aesthetic in one page-turn.
