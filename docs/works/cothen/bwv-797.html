Core idea. Flowing g-minor melancholy in the set's most seamless three-voice weave — no subject theatrics, just continuous elegiac motion passed among the parts, the g-minor invention's suspension-feeling now fully upholstered.

Study. The legato of texture exercise: play so that no handoff between voices is audible as a seam — the sorrow should seem to belong to the whole fabric, not to any line. Then find the two or three true climaxes and grade everything else beneath them: minor-mode flow tempts uniform intensity; resist.
