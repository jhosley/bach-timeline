WTC I/19: Prelude and Fugue in A major

BWV 864 · A major ·

The pair. The role-reversal pair: the prelude is itself a strict three-voice fughetta — imitative from its first bar — while the official three-voice fugue sports one of the book's oddest subjects: it starts with a leap and a silence, a stuttering figure the counterpoint then treats with perfect seriousness.

Study. With I/7, the form-boundary exhibit. And a subject-design lesson: a fugue subject needs identity more than beauty — this one's hiccup makes every later entry unmissable. Compare the featureless-but-noble Cathedral subject: two opposite solutions to recognizability.
