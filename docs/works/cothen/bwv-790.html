Sinfonia No. 4 in D minor

BWV 790 · D minor · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. Grave, poised D minor: two upper voices in close, sighing imitation over a bass of steady tread — the texture of a slow trio-sonata movement compressed onto ten fingers, dissonances leaning and resolving in chains.

Study. The three-voice suspension study (No. 11's lesson, upgraded): with three voices, dissonance becomes chordal — identify who holds, who moves, who grounds. Then the expressive calibration: how much lean is grief, how much is mannerism? Deciding is the interpreter's job this page assigns.
