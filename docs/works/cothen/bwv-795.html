Core idea. The masterpiece of the thirty, by common consent — and one of the darkest short pieces Bach ever wrote: three distinct subjects (a halting, sigh-broken figure; a chromatic descent; a winding lament) combined in triple counterpoint, meaning any subject can sit in any voice — and across the piece they rotate through the permutations like grief examined from every side. A Passion movement's substance in a teaching book.

Study. Name the three subjects A, B, C and log their combinations bar by bar — the piece is a permutation table with a broken heart. Then step back and take the larger lesson, the set's deepest: in Bach, maximum system and maximum sorrow are the same page. Everything the Craft essays argue is proven here, in under four minutes, by homework.
