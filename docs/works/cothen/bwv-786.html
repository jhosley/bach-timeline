Core idea. The set's exit exam and its most fugue-like page: a crisp subject featuring a written-out trill figure — ornament promoted to structure — treated with an almost strict imitative discipline. B minor, the WTC's terminal key and the Mass's, closing the two-part course with a glance at the road ahead.

Study. Graduation exercise: label it as if it were a fugue exposition (subject, answer, episodes) and see how far the labels hold. Where they break down is exactly the difference between invention and fugue — which is the next course: the Sinfonias, then the WTC.
