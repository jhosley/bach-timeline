The pair. The unique one: the only two-voice fugue in all forty-eight — essentially a fierce invention at fugue's address, driving to a unison finish — preceded by a prelude that morphs mid-piece: an ornamented lament over a pulsing bass that suddenly bolts into presto.

Study. The bridge piece between your Inventions work and the fugues proper: ask what makes this a fugue rather than a sixteenth invention (answer: the subject/answer key-relationship and episode logic — the skeleton, not the voice-count). Genre is architecture, not headcount.
