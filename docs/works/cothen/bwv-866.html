WTC I/21: Prelude and Fugue in B-flat major

BWV 866 · B-flat major ·
documented
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

The pair. The virtuoso pair: a toccata-prelude of hand-crossing flourishes and cadenza sweeps — the young performer's Bach (the Chromatic Fantasia's little cousin) let loose inside the disciplined book — then a three-voice fugue whose leaping, long-limbed subject keeps the brilliance airborne.

Study. Performance-history note: this prelude's improvisatory glitter is what Bach's own playing reportedly sounded like in free moments. Play the pair as display and it works; find the fugue's inner-voice craft anyway. Both truths at once — that's the whole instrument-side of Bach.
