The pair. A grave little prelude with an old vocal-polyphony soul, then a four-voice fugue whose trill-marked subject carries a striking family resemblance to the choral fugue "Es ist der alte Bund" in the Actus tragicus — "it is the ancient law: man, thou must die" — the funeral cantata's gravity visiting the keyboard book.

Study. Chase the resemblance yourself (both works are on this timeline): whether quotation or shared formula, it opens the right question — how much of Bach's "abstract" music carries texted associations his first players would have heard at once?
