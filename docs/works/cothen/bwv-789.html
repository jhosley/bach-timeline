Core idea. Festive D major with the energy of its two-part sibling raised to the third power: bright imitative entries, sweeping sequences, and a bass line that refuses to be a mere floor — it takes the subject as often as the upper voices do.

Study. Watch the bass: count its thematic entries. The Sinfonias' deepest upgrade over the Inventions is the emancipated bass — foundation and melody at once — and this piece is its cleanest demonstration. (That double-duty bass is the engine of the gamba sonatas and all Bach's trio textures.)
