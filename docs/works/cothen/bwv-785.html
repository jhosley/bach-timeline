Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Invention No. 14 in B-flat major

BWV 785 · B-flat major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The craftsman's invention: an amiable subject built from turning broken figures, developed with the set's most even-handed thoroughness — no drama, no dance, just two voices in perfectly courteous conversation, every gesture answered.

Study. Use it for the dialogue exercise: play it as two characters and decide, phrase by phrase, who is proposing and who is responding — then reverse your casting and hear how it still works. Counterpoint as conversation is not a metaphor here; it's the construction method.
