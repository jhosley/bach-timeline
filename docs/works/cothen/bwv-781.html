Cöthen: the instrumental golden age

Invention No. 10 in G major

BWV 781 · G major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. A gigue in disguise: compound-meter lilt, open-air G major, the two voices skipping in and out of imitation — the dance-suite world (the French Suites share this shelf-year) visiting the counterpoint classroom.

Study. The meter lesson: feel it in the big beat (the lilt), not the small notes, and the piece plays itself. Bach's dances live or die by step-feel — learn to find it here, at minimum difficulty, before the cello suite gigues demand it at maximum.
