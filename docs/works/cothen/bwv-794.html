Sinfonia No. 8 in F major

BWV 794 · F major · 1723
documented
Forces — Keyboard (three voices)
Recommended recording — see set card  ·  Recordings Shelf
Score — IMSLP (free score)

Core idea. The F-major chase with a third runner: robust, bright, close-imitative — entries stacking on each other's heels, the three voices tumbling through sequences with the set's most extroverted energy. The nearest a Sinfonia comes to a fughetta in spirit.

Study. Entry-spotting at speed: mark every subject entrance and play the piece bringing only those to the front. This is exactly the fugue-listener's core skill, drilled at friendly tempo — the WTC assumes you own it.
