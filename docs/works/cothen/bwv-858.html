The pair. Six sharps of contentment: a gently rocking prelude in duet texture, then a three-voice fugue of deliberately free design — no canon, no inversion, just an amiable ornament-tipped subject strolled through a texture that never raises its voice. With I/3, the proof that the "new" remote keys inspired Bach to ease, not display.

Study. A performance study more than an analytical one: sustaining interest without drama. What holds attention here — timbral registers? phrase overlap? harmonic pace? Name it, and you've learned something about the other forty-seven too.
