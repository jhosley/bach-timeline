About This Companion

The Well-Tempered Companion is a personal, non-commercial study companion to the life, works, and afterlife of Johann Sebastian Bach, created and maintained by James Edmund Hosley II as an aid to private study. It is offered free of charge, carries no advertising, and sells nothing.

Relationship to the reference works

This site is a companion to — not a substitute for — the scholarly works listed in its bibliography. The biographical narratives, dating judgments, and interpretive framing throughout draw on those published sources, which are cited on every card. Students who wish to go deeper are strongly encouraged to purchase and read the referenced books — above all Christoph Wolff's Johann Sebastian Bach: The Learned Musician, The New Bach Reader, and John Eliot Gardiner's Music in the Castle of Heaven — and to support the scholars, performers, and institutions whose work makes a resource like this possible.

Sources, images, and scores

  • Historical images are reproduced from Wikimedia Commons, NASA, and archive.org under public-domain status or the Creative Commons licenses credited in each caption. Photographer and license attributions accompany every Creative Commons image.
  • Score PDFs are public-domain editions (chiefly the Bach-Gesellschaft Ausgabe, 1851–1899, and other pre-1930 editions) obtained from the International Music Score Library Project (IMSLP) and archive.org, hosted here for study convenience.
  • Quotations from modern scholarly works are brief, attributed, and made for purposes of commentary, scholarship, and education.
