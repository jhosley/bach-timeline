Eisenach: born into the guild

Birth of Johann Sebastian Bach

1685-03-21 · eisenach
documented

Johann Sebastian Bach was born on 21 March 1685, the eighth and youngest child of Johann Ambrosius Bach, director of Eisenach's town music, and Elisabeth née Lämmerhirt. He was baptized on 23 March at the Georgenkirche; the register survives. His godfathers — a town musician and a ducal forestry official — mark the family's exact social station: solid, guild-respectable, nowhere near gentry.

A note on the date. Protestant Germany still used the old Julian calendar in 1685; by the modern (Gregorian) calendar the date is 31 March. This timeline gives old-style dates for events inside Germany before 1700, as the sources do, and flags conversions where they matter.

Why it matters. 1685 is the great vintage year of the Baroque — see the world-track card for the two exact contemporaries, Handel and Domenico Scarlatti, whose paths make Bach's chosen narrowness (he alone never left Germany, never wrote an opera) so illuminating by contrast. And the birthplace matters as much as the date: to be born a Bach, in Luther's town, in musical Thuringia, was to inherit all three of the forces — craft, faith, and region — that this timeline's tracks follow for the next sixty-five years.
