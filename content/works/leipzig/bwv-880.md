**The pair.** The spacious one: a prelude of broad, organ-like sonority — five-voice textures rolling in slow waves, unique in the books for sheer acreage — then a light-footed **three-voice fugue** whose **final bass entry famously "stumbles,"** adding an extra leap to the subject like a dancer taking one liberty too many, on purpose, at the bow.

**Study.** Documented Bach humor, notated ([the Coffee Cantata's](bwv-211.md) instrumental cousin): the joke only lands if you know the subject cold — which is the pedagogical trick: the punchline *tests the listener's* fugal ear. Late Bach smiling at his own audience of connoisseurs.
