**The pair.** Brilliance before the end: a sparkling toccata-prelude, then a **double fugue opening in *stile antico*** — a calm, white-note first subject straight from the old church, joined mid-course by a running second subject that floods the antique frame with modern motion, the two styles finally sounding together.

**Study.** The book's thesis in one fugue: old and new not as before/after but as *counterpoint to each other* — literally combined. The same gesture, at cathedral scale, closes [the B minor Mass's Credo](b-minor-mass.md); hear this first as the chamber rehearsal.
