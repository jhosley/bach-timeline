**What it is.** Week two of the Leipzig era, and the twin of [BWV 75](bwv-75.md): same fourteen-movement, two-part plan, same chorale-frame architecture — the pair clearly designed as a double portal to the new regime. Psalm 19 opens it: "The heavens declare the glory of God" — trumpet and choir in bright C-major proclamation, the firmament as fanfare.

**Listen for.** The opening's two-stage design (festive proclamation, then a striding fugue on "there is no speech nor language"); and Part II's shift of world: after the sermon, an intimate **sinfonia with oboe d'amore and viola da gamba** — the cosmos scaled down to chamber inwardness, the day's public and private theologies in one work.

**Why it matters.** Completes the inaugural diptych: hear 75 and 76 back to back (as Leipzig did, two Sundays running) and you hear a composer *announcing a program* — scale, structure, chorale-thinking — that [the next two years](../../events/leipzig-1/cantata-machine.md) delivered at unimaginable pace.
