**The pair.** Book II opens by measuring the distance from [Book I's arpeggio-halo](../cothen/bwv-846.md): this C-major prelude is a *composition* — a spacious, fully-voiced movement over striding octaves, grown from an earlier draft Bach expanded — and the **three-voice fugue** rides a terse fanfare-figure to a spirited coda.

**Study.** The two books' openers, back to back, are the fastest possible lesson in early vs. late Bach: harmony-as-atmosphere versus harmony-as-architecture. Start any Book II study exactly here, with that comparison in your ears.
