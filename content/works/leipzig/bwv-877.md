**The pair.** Six sharps of severity: an intense two-part prelude in perpetual dialogue, then a **ricercar-style four-voice fugue** — the archaic, research-grade species (the word the [Musical Offering](musical-offering.md) resurrects) — working its subject through **inversion and canon** with the concentrated learning of the coming late monuments.

**Study.** The proto-[Art-of-Fugue](art-of-fugue.md): hear this as a study for that project's severities, written into the "teaching" book. Compare its enharmonic sibling in [Book I (eb/d#)](../cothen/bwv-853.md) — the same tonal place, lament then discipline.
