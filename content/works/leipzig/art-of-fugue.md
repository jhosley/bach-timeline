**What it is.** [The event card](../../events/leipzig-3/art-of-fugue-begun.md) tells the biography; here is the music: fourteen fugues and four canons, every one grown from a single plain D-minor subject — simple fugues, counter-fugues on its inversion, double and triple fugues, **mirror fugues** (two complete fugues, one the other's exact reflection), the strictness ratcheting movement by movement until the unfinished quadruple summit where **B–A–C–H** enters as the third subject and the music stops mid-air.

**Listen for, as a journey.** Contrapunctus 1: the subject bare, the rules stated. 4: the inversion singing on its own. 9: the racing double fugue everyone loves first. 11: the triple fugue's chromatic storm — the cycle's expressive extreme. The mirror pair (12–13): listen to both halves back to back; your ear learns what "inverted world" means. Then 14, and the silence.

**Listen for, as sound.** Choose your instrument-question: keyboard (the scholarly consensus — one mind, one sitting), string quartet or viols (the voices embodied), even orchestra. The open score *invites* the question; three centuries of answers are part of the work now.

**Craft.** The R4 fugue essay's final chapter — and the Craft section's whole thesis ([one idea, total development](../cothen/inventions-sinfonias.md)) at terminal scale.

**Reception thread.** Sold ~30 copies; plates nearly melted for copper — then the twentieth century made it a monument of pure mind: the emblem of Bach-as-structure that Arc III's modernists claimed for their own.
