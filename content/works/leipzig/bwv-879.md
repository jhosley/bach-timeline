**The pair.** A flowing two-part prelude (binary, dance-blooded), then a **three-voice fugue** built on the **longest subject in either book — six full bars** of winding, chromatic-shaded melody: less a motto than a paragraph, whose every later entry is an event simply because of its span.

**Study.** Subject-length as form: with a six-bar subject, episodes shrink and entries dominate — the whole fugal economy inverts. Compare the four-note [I/4 subject](../cothen/bwv-849.md): the two extremes of subject design, both masterpieces. (And recall [I/10](../cothen/bwv-855.md), E minor's *other* extremity — this key collects the book's outliers.)
