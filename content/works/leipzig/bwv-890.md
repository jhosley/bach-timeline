**The pair.** The expansive charmer: one of the longest preludes in either book — a rolling, sonata-like movement whose two-part games run to real developmental length (the classical era knocking) — paired with a **three-voice fugue in minuet character**: courtly triple-time grace under fugal law.

**Study.** Another dance-fugue for the collection ([passepied I/11](../cothen/bwv-856.md), [gavotte II/13](bwv-882.md), gigue [II/4](bwv-873.md), now minuet) — assemble the set and you have Bach's demonstration that *every* social dance can think contrapuntally. The R4 dance essay builds its keyboard section on exactly this list.
