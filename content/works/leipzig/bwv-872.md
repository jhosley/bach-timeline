**The pair.** The remote key revisited a generation on: a luminous prelude (adapted, tellingly, from an earlier C-major draft — Bach transposing *into* the once-impossible key as a matter of course now), and a quicksilver **three-voice fugue** whose subject is nothing but **notes of the triad, constantly mutated** — minimal material, maximal play.

**Study.** Motivic economy at the far end of the career: compare [Invention 1's](../cothen/bwv-772.md) seven-note thrift. Twenty-five years later the game is the same and the touch is lighter — late Bach's virtuosity is *reduction*.
