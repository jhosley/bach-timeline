**The pair.** Five sharps of quicksilver unease: a long, restless two-part prelude, then a **double fugue** — first subject nervous and darting; **second subject a slow chromatic descent** (the lament-line, [F minor's old freight](../cothen/bwv-857.md)) — the two finally combined so the fidget and the grief are revealed as one music.

**Study.** Double-fugue listening in three stages: know subject A, know subject B, then attend only to their combination section — where late Bach does his deepest thinking. This one and [II/14](bwv-883.md) are Book II's paired courses in multi-subject form: take both.
