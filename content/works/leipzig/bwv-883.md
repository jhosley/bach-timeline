**The pair.** Book II's inner sanctum: an arioso prelude of refined melancholy, then the book's **only triple fugue** — three subjects (the first austere, the second flowing, the third a chain of two-note sighs), each exposed separately, then **combined** in the closing pages with valedictory calm rather than triumph.

**Study.** The gentlest door into multi-subject architecture before [the St. Anne](bwv-552.md) and [Art of Fugue](art-of-fugue.md): three ideas, one texture, no noise. Learn the three subjects by ear first; the ending then plays like three old friends agreeing at last. Many pianists' private favorite of the forty-eight.
