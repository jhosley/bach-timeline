**The pair.** Book II's benediction: a pastoral prelude, then the celebrated ***stile antico* four-voice fugue** — its subject modeled on the E-major fugue of **Fischer's *Ariadne Musica*** (the modest pre-Bach cycle of preludes and fugues through many keys: Bach bowing to the whole project's ancestor), moving in white-note calm like Renaissance vocal polyphony transcribed from eternity.

**Study.** The homage tells you how Bach read history: he *out-composes* his model while honoring it — the same gesture as [the Reincken improvisation](../../events/cothen/hamburg-reincken-1720.md). Many rank this fugue among the most perfect pages in either book; test the claim slowly.
