**The pair.** A lilting, lute-like prelude of unbroken grace — one of Book II's most loved — and an ***alla breve* four-voice fugue**: the old church meter (the flowing two-beat of Renaissance polyphony), its serene subject moving in the long values of the *stile antico* family.

**Study.** File with the book's antique wing ([II/9](bwv-878.md), [II/23](bwv-892.md)): late Bach curating music history inside the 24-key frame, exactly as [the B minor Mass](b-minor-mass.md) does with sacred styles. The WTC as museum — Book II's distinctive dimension.
