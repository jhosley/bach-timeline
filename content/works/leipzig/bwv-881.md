**The pair.** F minor, the grief-key ([I/12](../cothen/bwv-857.md), [the Sinfonia](../cothen/bwv-795.md)) — revisited in the *galant* generation's accents: a binary-form prelude of sighing two-note figures and empfindsam (sensitive-style) pathos that could almost be by his son Emanuel, then a **three-voice fugue** whose energy is nervous rather than tragic.

**Study.** Late Bach absorbing the *new* style he supposedly opposed ([Scheibe's](../../events/leipzig-2/scheibe-1737.md) actual complaint, answered by assimilation): the prelude is the book's clearest bridge to the coming classical era. Compare the three F-minor griefs across twenty years — vocabulary changes, the address doesn't.
