**The pair.** Orchestral majesty on two staves: a prelude that struts like a trumpet-and-strings concerto movement, and a **four-voice fugue** of ceremonious power whose climax — around bar 44 — **combines subject statements in all four voices**: the full company on stage at once.

**Study.** Stretto as arrival: the all-voices pile-up is the fugue's *destination*, prepared like a cadence. Trace how the earlier, partial strettos rehearse it. The D-major public manner ([orchestral suites](bwv-1068.md), [Magnificat](magnificat.md)) distilled to ten fingers.
