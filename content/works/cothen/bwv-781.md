**Core idea.** A gigue in disguise: compound-meter lilt, open-air G major, the two voices skipping in and out of imitation — the dance-suite world ([the French Suites](french-suites.md) share this shelf-year) visiting the counterpoint classroom.

**Study.** The meter lesson: feel it in the big beat (the lilt), not the small notes, and the piece plays itself. Bach's dances live or die by *step-feel* — learn to find it here, at minimum difficulty, before the [cello suite gigues](cello-suites.md) demand it at maximum.
