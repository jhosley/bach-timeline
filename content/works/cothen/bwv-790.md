**Core idea.** Grave, poised D minor: two upper voices in close, sighing imitation over a bass of steady tread — the texture of a slow trio-sonata movement compressed onto ten fingers, dissonances leaning and resolving in chains.

**Study.** The three-voice suspension study ([No. 11's lesson](bwv-782.md), upgraded): with three voices, dissonance becomes *chordal* — identify who holds, who moves, who grounds. Then the expressive calibration: how much lean is grief, how much is mannerism? Deciding is the interpreter's job this page assigns.
