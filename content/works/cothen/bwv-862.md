**The pair.** Four flats of nobility: a spacious concerto-gesture prelude, then the **four-voice fugue** German tradition nicknamed the *Cathedral Fugue* — a plain, bell-like subject built to sound grander at each entry, the texture accumulating like vaulting.

**Study.** How does plainness become grandeur? The subject is almost featureless; the *architecture* — entry spacing, register climb, episode restraint — supplies everything. Reverse-engineer the crescendo of dignity: it's structural, not dynamic, and that lesson transfers to playing all late Bach.
