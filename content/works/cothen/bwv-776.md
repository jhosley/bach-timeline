**Core idea.** The courtly one: a gracious, ornamented melody over an active companion line, the two in constant **double counterpoint** — meaning the parts are built to swap floors (melody moves to the bass, accompaniment to the treble) and still sound right. E-flat's warmth, in the set's most galant manners.

**Study.** Find a passage, then find its upside-down twin (same music, voices exchanged). Writing lines that survive the swap is the invention-writer's core discipline — and the direct entry requirement for fugue, where every voice must take every job.
