**The pair.** A brooding prelude of triplet figuration over a restless bass — D-minor storm-weather at candle-flame size — and a sinewy **three-voice fugue** whose snaking subject works constantly against its own **inversion**, without ever settling into a fixed countersubject: improvisatory logic under strict control.

**Study.** Mark upright vs. inverted entries in two colors; the piece becomes a woven pattern before your eyes. And note the ending — the last bars pile up chromatic tension and resolve with a picardy blaze: miniature dramaturgy worth stealing for performance pacing.
