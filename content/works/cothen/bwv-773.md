**Core idea.** A long, singing minor-mode line — and a hidden discipline: for most of its length the left hand follows the right in **strict canon at the octave**, two bars behind. Most students play it for years without noticing; Bach teaching canon before naming it.

**Study.** Play the right hand alone, then start the left two bars later — hear the canon bare. Then ask the craft question the piece exists to pose: how must a melody be *built* so that it can accompany itself? ([The Goldberg canons](../leipzig/goldberg-variations.md) are this lesson's far horizon.)
