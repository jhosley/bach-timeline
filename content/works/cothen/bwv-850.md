**The pair.** A whirring perpetual-motion prelude (right hand spinning, left hand striding), then the book's costume piece: a **four-voice fugue in French-overture dress** — dotted rhythms, ceremonial snap, the royal-entrance genre ([BWV 61's trick](../weimar/bwv-61.md)) compressed into fugal miniature.

**Study.** The style-reference lesson: a fugue can *impersonate* a genre. Inventory the French signals (double-dotting, tirades — the upward scale-flicks) and then note the fugue is structurally strict beneath the costume. Style and structure are separate layers in Bach; this pair makes the separation audible.
