**Core idea.** The elegy of the two-part set: a compact, ornament-inflected figure whose written turns and trills are not decoration but the *substance* of the line — pathos carried in small curls of melody over a darker companion voice.

**Study.** Consult [the ornament table Bach wrote for Friedemann](../../events/cothen/clavierbuchlein-wf-1720.md) — this piece is its application exam. Play the line stripped of ornaments, then restored: what exactly do the ornaments *add*? (Answer: the speech. Baroque ornament is diction, not jewelry.)
