**Core idea.** The concert favorite among the minor-key inventions: a restless arpeggiated subject answered immediately in the other hand, then driven through the set's most sweeping sequences — two pages that feel symphonic. The romantics loved it; it survives on recital programs where the others live in studios.

**Study.** A form lesson: find the three big harmonic stations (home minor — relative major — home again) and hear the piece as one journey with two turns. The Inventions' binary-ish tonal plan is the seed of every sonata form your later studies will meet.
