**Core idea.** The G-major gigue's shadowed twin: flowing, serious, its expressive weight carried by **suspensions** — one voice holding its note while the other moves against it, creating the dissonance that must lean and resolve. The set's study in two-voice *harmony as feeling*.

**Study.** Circle the suspensions; play each as three acts (preparation — dissonance — resolution) and exaggerate the lean. Suspension-hearing unlocks the sarabandes, the slow movements, and half the grief in Bach; this page is where to acquire it.
