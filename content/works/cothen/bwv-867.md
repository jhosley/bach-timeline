**The pair.** The second summit ([I/4's](bwv-849.md) sibling): a prelude of tolling solemnity, then the book's other **five-voice fugue** — its subject leaping a raw **minor ninth** and then *pausing*, a rhetorical catch of breath built into the theme itself, before five voices carry it through strettos of gathering intensity to a colossal final entry.

**Study.** The subject *is* the lesson: one expressive interval plus one silence, and the whole edifice inherits their gravity. The five-voice pair (I/4 and I/22) belongs at the center of any WTC study plan — the book's claim to sacred-music weight, made instrumentally.
