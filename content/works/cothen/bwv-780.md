**Core idea.** The tragedy of the two-part set: F minor — Bach's key of deepest grief (the [WTC's](wtc-1.md) F-minor pair, the f-minor Sinfonia) — with chromatic descents and suspensions carrying real anguish in a children's exercise book. Two voices are enough for sorrow; that is the lesson.

**Study.** Trace the descending chromatic lines (the [lament-bass vocabulary](../weimar/bwv-12.md) in miniature) and the chains of dissonance-resolution. Then the pedagogical shock: this stands three pages from [the F-major chase](bwv-779.md) — the affections as *adjacent teachable techniques*, which is the whole Baroque aesthetic in one page-turn.
