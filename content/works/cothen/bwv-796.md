**Core idea.** Sunlight after [the f-minor abyss](bwv-795.md) — the ordering is surely pedagogy of the spirit as well as the hands: buoyant G major, a nimble triadic subject, three voices at play in the set's most uncomplicated happiness.

**Study.** Recovery repertoire, and a balance etude: keep the dance-lightness while giving each of three voices a real presence — lightness in counterpoint is *earned*, never default. Compare the [G-major two-part gigue](bwv-781.md): same key-character, one voice richer, how much heavier does it want to become? Resist exactly that.
