**Core idea.** Serious C minor in continuous flowing motion — the three voices passing a supple sixteenth-note figure among themselves over a steadily walking foundation, the texture never resting, the mood never lifting into brightness.

**Study.** The *handoff* exercise: the moving figure migrates voice to voice — mark every handoff and make each one audible (the arriving voice slightly forward, the yielding voice stepping back). Textural spotlighting, learned here, is precisely what [the organ trio sonatas](../leipzig/trio-sonatas-organ.md) and every fugue will demand.
