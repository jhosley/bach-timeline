**Core idea.** Warm A-major confidence: an assured subject worked through the three voices with the set's most even craftsmanship — the piece that *sounds* like what the title page promised the graduate would possess: fluency so complete it reads as ease.

**Study.** The transposition exercise the old pedagogy would assign: take its opening bars and write them in three nearby keys, keeping all three voices correct. Nothing exposes whether you understand a texture (versus merely finger it) faster — and the Sinfonias are the right difficulty for the attempt.
