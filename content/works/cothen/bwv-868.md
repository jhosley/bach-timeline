**The pair.** Five sharps of benediction before the finale's storm: a warm, compact prelude and a **four-voice fugue** — the only four-voice fugue of Book I cast in **binary form** (two halves, sectional like a dance movement): fugue and suite quietly shaking hands.

**Study.** Formal hybridity again, at the gentlest scale (compare the [passepied-fugue](bwv-856.md) and [overture-fugue](bwv-850.md)). And placement-listening: hear it in sequence between the massive [b-flat minor](bwv-867.md) and [B minor](bwv-869.md) pairs — the book's pacing, like a Passion's, plants calm before catastrophe.
