**Core idea.** The third voice arrives. Same C-major world as [Invention No. 1](bwv-772.md) — rising scale-figure DNA, plain daylight key — but now three lines share it, and the middle voice changes everything: no longer dialogue but *society*, each part taking turns as melody, companion, and bass.

**Study.** The Sinfonia fundamentals, learned here first: play each voice alone until it sings; then each *pair*; only then all three. And the harder discipline — at any moment, know which voice is the *subject* and which two are accompanying. That triage is three-part playing's whole secret.
