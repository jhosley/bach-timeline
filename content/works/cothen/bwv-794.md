**Core idea.** The [F-major chase](bwv-779.md) with a third runner: robust, bright, close-imitative — entries stacking on each other's heels, the three voices tumbling through sequences with the set's most extroverted energy. The nearest a Sinfonia comes to a fughetta in spirit.

**Study.** Entry-spotting at speed: mark every subject entrance and play the piece bringing *only* those to the front. This is exactly the fugue-listener's core skill, drilled at friendly tempo — [the WTC](wtc-1.md) assumes you own it.
