**Core idea.** A swirling sixteenth-note figure spun from the D-minor triad, driving and dramatic — the teaching book's first taste of the *storm* register (D minor is [the Chaconne's](bwv-1004.md) and [the Art of Fugue's](../leipzig/art-of-fugue.md) key, and the weather starts here). The figure works as hard **inverted** as upright.

**Study.** Find where the motive flips; then notice the long dominant preparation before the final cadence — tension held deliberately. Small piece, real dramaturgy: ask *where the climax is* and how you know.
