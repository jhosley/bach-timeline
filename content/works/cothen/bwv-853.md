**The pair.** Book I's tragic center. The prelude: a sarabande-lament of rolled chords and keening melody — grief in six flats, among the deepest pages Bach wrote for keyboard. The **three-voice fugue** is notated *enharmonically* in D-sharp minor (same sounds, six sharps — a well-tempering demonstration in the orthography itself) and unfolds with old-style gravity, its subject examined in inversion and augmentation as the lament turns learned.

**Study.** The pair to sit with slowly. For craft: the fugue's augmentation entries (the subject at half speed, like grief slowing time). For the timeline: this is the emotional benchmark [Book II's B-flat minor pair](../leipzig/bwv-891.md) answers a generation later.
