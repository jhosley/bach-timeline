**Core idea.** The rhythm lesson: one voice moves in steady notes while the other **syncopates against it** — tied notes leaning across the beat — and then the hands trade jobs. Serene E-major surface; under it, a precise study in rhythmic independence. (Also one of the few inventions in two repeated halves, like a little dance-suite movement.)

**Study.** Tap one hand's rhythm while singing the other's. The independence this piece drills — two rhythmic characters, one player — is the same skill [the organ trio sonatas](../leipzig/trio-sonatas-organ.md) will demand at full difficulty.
