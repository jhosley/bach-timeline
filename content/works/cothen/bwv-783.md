**Core idea.** Radiant and athletic: an ornament-launched arpeggio figure that keeps unfolding across the keyboard's registers, the two voices exchanging brilliance rather than argument. A study in **figuration** — how a harmonic skeleton is dressed in running motion.

**Study.** Do the reduction: strip each bar to its underlying chord and write the progression out. Then watch how the figure *is* the chord, animated. That harmony-behind-the-motion X-ray is the analytical skill this piece exists to build — apply it next to [the first cello suite's prelude](bwv-1007.md).
