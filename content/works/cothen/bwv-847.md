**The pair.** The motor-rhythm prelude — a two-hand toccata of churning sixteenths that suddenly breaks into free cadenza before the end (Presto–Adagio–Allegro: a drama in three tempo words) — and a crisp **three-voice fugue** on a dancing, mordent-flecked subject, closing with a Picardy-third smile.

**Study.** The fugue is many teachers' first-assigned fugue for good reason: textbook exposition, clearly separated episodes, audible subject entries throughout. Map it completely once and you own the vocabulary for the other 47.
