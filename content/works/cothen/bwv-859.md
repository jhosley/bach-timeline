**The pair.** A busy, slightly anxious two-part prelude, then one of Book I's under-sung treasures: a **four-voice fugue** whose long-breathed subject sings rather than argues — Hewitt calls out its unusual lyric beauty — the four voices carrying it like an ensemble of violas.

**Study.** The *cantabile fugue*: prove to yourself it can be phrased like an aria (sing the subject; find its breath). Then note the key's future: [Book II's F-sharp minor](../leipzig/bwv-883.md) will build the only triple fugue of that book on this same tonal ground — melancholy deepened by twenty years.
