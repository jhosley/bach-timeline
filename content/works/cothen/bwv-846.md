**The pair.** The most famous first page in keyboard music: a prelude of nothing but arpeggiated chords — pure harmony in motion, [the C-major cello prelude's](bwv-1007.md) keyboard twin — followed by a serene **four-voice fugue** whose stretto-rich texture keeps the subject overlapping itself like calm ripples.

**Study.** Write out the prelude as block chords (Bach's own students did) — it is a figured-bass lesson wearing a halo. **Reception note:** Gounod laid an *Ave Maria* melody over the prelude in 1853 — Arc II's Romantic Bach, literally superimposed on the original.
