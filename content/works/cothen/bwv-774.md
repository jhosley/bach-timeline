**Core idea.** D-major brightness in compound-meter swing: a lilting figure that rides long **sequences** (the same idea restated a step higher or lower — Bach's basic engine of momentum) toward each cadence. The happiest of the fifteen.

**Study.** Bracket the sequences: where does each begin, how many rungs does the ladder have, where does it land? Sequence-hearing is the single most transferable skill the Inventions teach — the concertos ([Brandenburgs](brandenburg-concertos.md) included) run on exactly this ladder-work at orchestra scale.
