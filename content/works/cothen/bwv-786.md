**Core idea.** The set's exit exam and its most fugue-like page: a crisp subject featuring a written-out **trill figure** — ornament promoted to structure — treated with an almost strict imitative discipline. B minor, [the WTC's](wtc-1.md) terminal key and [the Mass's](../leipzig/b-minor-mass.md), closing the two-part course with a glance at the road ahead.

**Study.** Graduation exercise: label it as if it were a fugue exposition (subject, answer, episodes) and see how far the labels hold. Where they break down is exactly the difference between invention and fugue — which is the next course: [the Sinfonias](bwv-787.md), then [the WTC](wtc-1.md).
