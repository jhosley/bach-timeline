**Core idea.** The graduation dance: a quick, glinting b-minor piece in an unusual whirling meter (9/16), its figuration flying between the voices with fugue-adjacent discipline at scherzo velocity — the course ending not in solemnity but in *earned play*.

**Study.** The last lesson is tempo-truth: the piece only works when the three-voice clarity you've built across thirty pieces survives at speed. If it does, the title page's promise is redeemed — and your next assignment, in Bach's own sequencing, is [a prelude and fugue in every key](wtc-1.md).
