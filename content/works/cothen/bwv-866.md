**The pair.** The virtuoso pair: a **toccata-prelude** of hand-crossing flourishes and cadenza sweeps — the young performer's Bach ([the Chromatic Fantasia's](bwv-903.md) little cousin) let loose inside the disciplined book — then a **three-voice fugue** whose leaping, long-limbed subject keeps the brilliance airborne.

**Study.** Performance-history note: this prelude's improvisatory glitter is what Bach's *own playing* reportedly sounded like in free moments. Play the pair as display and it works; find the fugue's inner-voice craft anyway. Both truths at once — that's the whole instrument-side of Bach.
