**Core idea.** The e-minor elegy scaled up from [its two-part sibling](bwv-778.md): a grieving, ornament-inflected subject moving through three voices, the texture darkening as lines cross and gather — the teaching book's first sustained *tragic* three-part span, preparing the greater grief [two keys away](bwv-795.md).

**Study.** Register the difference a third voice makes to sorrow: in two parts, grief is a duet; in three it becomes a *space* — someone is always sustaining while others move, like held breath under speech. That sustained-voice awareness is the skill; carry it to the [f-minor Sinfonia](bwv-795.md) next.
