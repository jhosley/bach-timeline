**Core idea.** Pastoral E major — the sharp-key serenity Bach reserved for radiance ([the E-major violin Partita's](bwv-1006.md) family) — with the three voices weaving in gentle compound motion, conflict entirely absent: counterpoint as concord, the discipline demonstrating its sweetest face.

**Study.** A voicing etude: keep all three lines *equal* here — no spotlighting — and discover how much control equality takes. Then compare with [No. 5's](bwv-791.md) aria texture: two adjacent pieces, two opposite balance problems. The set teaches by such pairings throughout.
