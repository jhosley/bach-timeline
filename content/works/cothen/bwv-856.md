**The pair.** Lightness squared: a rippling two-voice prelude in moto perpetuo, then a **three-voice fugue that is secretly a passepied** — the quick French dance in 3/8 — its subject skipping through the texture with the ballroom in its step.

**Study.** Another genre-in-disguise (compare the [French-overture fugue, I/5](bwv-850.md)): the dance essay's evidence that Bach's "abstract" counterpoint constantly carries dance DNA. Find the passepied's characteristic lilt and let it set your tempo — the counterpoint will follow.
