**The pair.** E-major pastoral ([the family key of radiance](bwv-1006.md)): a lilting siciliano-tinged prelude, then a compact **three-voice fugue** of sunny thoroughness — ten subject entries, every one answered *exactly* (a "real" answer, unaltered by the key change), the theory-textbook case of the species.

**Study.** Real vs. tonal answers, learned painlessly: here every answer is real; then open [I/2](bwv-847.md) or almost any other fugue and find the adjusted (tonal) answers. One comparison, and a piece of fugue-jargon becomes a thing your ear owns.
