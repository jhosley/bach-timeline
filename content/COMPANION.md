# About This Companion

*The Well-Tempered Companion* is a personal, non-commercial study companion to the life, works, and afterlife of Johann Sebastian Bach, created and maintained by James Edmund Hosley II as an aid to private study. It is offered free of charge, carries no advertising, and sells nothing.

## Relationship to the reference works

This site is a **companion to — not a substitute for — the scholarly works listed in its [bibliography](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md)**. The biographical narratives, dating judgments, and interpretive framing throughout draw on those published sources, which are cited on every card. Students who wish to go deeper are strongly encouraged to **purchase and read the referenced books** — above all Christoph Wolff's *Johann Sebastian Bach: The Learned Musician*, *The New Bach Reader*, and John Eliot Gardiner's *Music in the Castle of Heaven* — and to support the scholars, performers, and institutions whose work makes a resource like this possible.

## Independence

All outbound links — to booksellers, record labels, streaming and video platforms, IMSLP, Bach Digital, the Netherlands Bach Society, and others — are provided solely for the reader's convenience. **This site has no affiliation, sponsorship, or commercial relationship of any kind with any linked party, and receives no royalty, commission, referral fee, or other consideration from any link.** Product and institution names are used for identification only and remain the property of their respective owners.

## Sources, images, and scores

- Historical images are reproduced from Wikimedia Commons, NASA, and archive.org under public-domain status or the Creative Commons licenses credited in each caption. Photographer and license attributions accompany every Creative Commons image.
- Score PDFs are public-domain editions (chiefly the Bach-Gesellschaft Ausgabe, 1851–1899, and other pre-1930 editions) obtained from the International Music Score Library Project (IMSLP) and archive.org, hosted here for study convenience.
- Quotations from modern scholarly works are brief, attributed, and made for purposes of commentary, scholarship, and education.

Should any rights holder believe material here exceeds fair use or misstates a license, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository and it will be promptly corrected or removed.
