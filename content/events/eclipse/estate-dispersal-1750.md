The *Specificatio* of Bach's estate survives — the notary's inventory of a prosperous burgher's household: mining shares, silver, eight harpsichords and ten other instruments, the **eighty-volume theological library**... and, listed almost in passing, the manuscripts: the largest body of unpublished masterworks any composer had left, divided among nine surviving heirs by ordinary inheritance law, like chairs.

The division's logic decided what the world would ever hear. **Carl Philipp Emanuel** — established at Frederick's court, careful, and pious toward his father's memory — took a great share including [the St. Matthew Passion](../../works/leipzig/st-matthew-passion.md) and [the B minor Mass](../../works/leipzig/b-minor-mass.md), preserved them, catalogued them, and lived long enough to hand them toward institutions. **Wilhelm Friedemann** — the adored eldest, [the Clavierbüchlein boy](../cothen/clavierbuchlein-wf-1720.md), by then beginning his long professional and personal unraveling — received among other things most of the chorale-cantata scores, and across three decades of increasing poverty **sold them off piecemeal**; most of what he held is gone. [Anna Magdalena](../../people/anna-magdalena-bach.md) gave her share of the [chorale-cantata performing parts](../../events/leipzig-1/cantata-machine.md) to the Thomasschule — which is precisely why that cycle survives better than the others: the one tributary that flowed into an institution instead of a market.

Scholars' sober arithmetic: perhaps **two-fifths of the cantatas**, at least two Passions, and untold instrumental works are simply lost — not burned in a dramatic fire but *dissolved*, sheet by sheet, through auction rooms and grocers' wrapping paper.

**Why it matters.** Arc II's material foundation: the eclipse wasn't only a failure of taste but a failure of *custody* — and every later scene on this ribbon ([Mozart with the parts spread around him](mozart-motet-1789.md), [Mendelssohn with a copyist's score](../revival/mendelssohn-revival-1829.md), [the Gesellschaft's 46 volumes](../revival/bach-gesellschaft-1850.md)) is someone rebuilding custody by hand.
