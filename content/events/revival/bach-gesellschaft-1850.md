On **28 July 1850** — one hundred years to the day after [the death in the Thomasschule apartment](../leipzig-3/death-1750.md) — a society constituted itself in Leipzig with a single stated object: to publish **the complete works of Johann Sebastian Bach**, critically edited from the sources, without arrangement or "improvement." Among the founders and first subscribers: **Robert Schumann** (whose journal had campaigned for exactly this for a decade), Moscheles, Otto Jahn; [Mendelssohn](../../people/felix-mendelssohn.md), the natural president, had died three years earlier — his revival work ([and the Leipzig Bach monument he personally financed in 1843](mendelssohn-revival-1829.md)) was the enterprise's visible foundation.

The undertaking consumed **fifty years and forty-six volumes** (1851–1899), and it is hard to overstate what it changed. [The manuscripts scattered in 1750](../eclipse/estate-dispersal-1750.md) were hunted, collated, and fixed in print; [the two hundred surviving cantatas](../leipzig-1/cantata-machine.md) — all but [one](../../works/muehlhausen/bwv-71.md) unpublished in Bach's lifetime — entered the public record; [the B minor Mass](../../works/leipzig/b-minor-mass.md) and [the unfinished Art of Fugue](../../works/leipzig/art-of-fugue.md) took their places as monuments. The edition's opening page gave [*Wie schön leuchtet der Morgenstern*](../../works/leipzig/bwv-1.md) its immortal number, and its completion moved **Brahms** — a subscriber who studied each volume as it arrived — to rank it with the founding of the German Empire among his era's great events (a comparison that says everything about [the national frame](../eclipse/forkel-1802.md) the whole project ran inside).

Every later instrument of Bach scholarship descends from this act: the BWV catalog numbers this timeline uses, the *Neue Bach-Ausgabe* that re-edited everything in the twentieth century, [Bach Digital's manuscript database](../../BIBLIOGRAPHY.md) — and every complete-cantata recording of [Arc III](../../eras/living-bach.md) is the Gesellschaft's shelf, performed.

**Why it matters.** Arc II ends where custody becomes *institution*: what one family lost, one nation's scholarship rebuilt — and from 1850 forward, Bach's survival never again depended on any single heir, attic, or accident. The timeline's ribbon, from here, only brightens.
