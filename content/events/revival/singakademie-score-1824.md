The revival's founding object is a birthday present. Around 1823–24, **Bella Salomon** — Felix [Mendelssohn](../../people/felix-mendelssohn.md)'s grandmother, of the banking family whose Berlin salon culture had kept "ancient music" alive for a generation — commissioned a complete **manuscript copy of the [St. Matthew Passion](../../works/leipzig/st-matthew-passion.md)** (from the materials in [Zelter's](../../eras/revival.md) Singakademie orbit) and gave it to her grandson: fourteen or fifteen years old, already a prodigious composer, already Zelter-trained in the strict old counterpoint.

Consider what the gift assumes: the greatest sacred work of the previous century existed **only in manuscript**, its reproduction a bespoke luxury purchase — and a Jewish-descended banking matriarch of Berlin was the one commissioning it, for a boy. (The Mendelssohn family's complex position — Moses Mendelssohn's grandchildren, converted to Protestantism, at once inside and outside the culture claiming Bach as [national patrimony](../eclipse/forkel-1802.md) — sits inside this scene, and Felix's later wry remark about "an actor and a Jew's son" reviving the great Christian music shows he knew it precisely.)

Felix lived with the score for five years — studying it, playing it at the family's Sunday musicales, gathering around it a cell of enthusiasts: above all **Eduard Devrient**, the baritone (and actor) whose memoirs preserve the campaign — the two young men in 1829 talking the skeptical old Zelter into lending his choir, his hall, and his blessing to [the performance the next card records](mendelssohn-revival-1829.md). Devrient sang Jesus; Felix conducted from memory.

**Why it matters.** Arc II's relay — [students to salon](../eclipse/van-swieten-salon.md), [salon to prodigy](../eclipse/beethoven-wtc.md) — reaches its terminal handoff: from custody to *performance*. And the object lesson stands for the whole reception story: revolutions of taste begin as private objects — a copied score, a gift, a room of friends — years before they have a public date.
